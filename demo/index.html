The model is a fine-tuned MobileNetV2 that scored 95.8% on photos it never saw in training (20 classes: 10 kinds of produce, fresh or rotten). Sample photos come from that held-out test set: the first photo of each class that has no stock-photo watermark, plus the hard calls: for each kind, the first photo the model knows the kind of but puts between fresh and rotten. The shelf life is the full app's rule of thumb (about a week when fresh, less as it rots), not a learned number.

The tiers, the "not sure" rule and the prices are the same as in the full app. The full app also finds each item in a live camera feed and forecasts spoilage; this page grades one photo.

The heat map ("what it looked at") is Grad-CAM, prepared in advance for the sample photos.
