Is this produce fresh?

Pick a photo or upload your own. The model looks at it and decides whether the item can go on the shelf at full price, should be marked down today, or should come off the shelf.

The model is a fine-tuned MobileNetV2 that scored 95.8% on photos it never saw in training (20 classes: 10 kinds of produce, fresh or rotten). Sample photos come from that held-out test set: the first photo of each class, plus one borderline photo (a fresh apple the model is unsure about) to show what a markdown looks like.

The tiers, the "not sure" rule and the prices are the same as in the full app. The full app also finds each item in a live camera feed and forecasts spoilage; this page grades one photo.

The heat map ("what it looked at") is Grad-CAM, prepared in advance for the sample photos.
